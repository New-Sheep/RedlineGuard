Section 14. Limitation of Liability

14.1 General Limitation

Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including loss of profits, revenue, data, or use, incurred by either party or any third party, whether in an action in contract or tort, even if the other party or any other person has been advised of the possibility of such damages.

14.2 Aggregate Liability Ceiling

The maximum aggregate liability of the Consultant to the Client for any and all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement, regardless of the form of action, shall in no event exceed the total amount of fees paid by the Client to the Consultant during the twelve (12) month period immediately preceding the event giving rise to such claim.

14.3 Carve-outs

The limitations of liability set forth in Section 14.1 and 14.2 shall not apply to (a) a party’s indemnification obligations; (b) breaches of confidentiality; or (c) damages caused by a party’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

Rewrite Decision

history Original Wording
"...in no event exceed the total amount of fees paid by the Client to the Consultant during the twelve (12) month period immediately preceding the event giving rise to such claim."
auto_fix_high Suggested Rewrite
"...in no event exceed three (3) times the total amount of fees paid or $500,000, whichever is greater."
info
Why this change

The original 12-month trailing fee cap is overly restrictive for a multi-year institutional engagement. Current market standards for high-value SaaS and consulting agreements typically benchmark against 2-3x multiples or fixed minimums to provide equitable protection for the Client.
