Master Services Agreement

Reference ID: RG-9928-LX

1. Engagement of Services

Provider shall perform the services described in each Statement of Work (SOW) executed by the parties. Each SOW shall be governed by the terms of this Agreement. Any conflict between this Agreement and a SOW shall be resolved in favor of the Agreement.

2. Compensation and Payment

Customer shall pay Provider the fees set forth in the applicable SOW. All invoices are due net thirty (30) days from receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

3. Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality or indemnification obligations, neither party's total aggregate liability shall exceed the total amount paid or payable by Customer to Provider under the applicable SOW during the 12 months preceding the claim. Provider explicitly waives any cap on damages resulting from intellectual property infringement claims.

4. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Cayman Islands, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any legal action arising under this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the courts of such jurisdiction.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice. Upon termination, Customer shall pay Provider for all services performed through the effective date of termination.

6. Confidentiality

During the term of this Agreement and for five years thereafter, each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other party's proprietary information. This includes but is not limited to trade secrets, customer lists, and technical specifications.

Risk Analysis

3 flags identified in current version

High Risk flag

Uncapped Indemnity

Section 3. Liability limits are missing for IP infringement, creating unlimited exposure.

The current wording excludes intellectual property claims from the liability cap. In the event of a patent or copyright dispute, the potential damages could exceed the contract's total value, posing a significant financial risk.

Medium Risk public

Odd Jurisdiction

Section 4. Jurisdiction set to Cayman Islands. High cost for litigation and unfamiliar legal framework.
