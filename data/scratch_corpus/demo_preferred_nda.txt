Mutual non-disclosure agreement (sample preferred paper).

1. Confidentiality. Each party shall protect Confidential Information for three (3) years
after termination, except Trade Secrets which remain protected while they qualify as such.
Perpetual confidentiality outside trade secrets is not accepted.

2. Governing law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.

3. Intellectual property. Each party retains all right, title and interest in its
pre-existing Intellectual Property. No assignment of background IP is granted.

4. Liability. Aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the preceding twelve months.
Unlimited liability is not accepted.
